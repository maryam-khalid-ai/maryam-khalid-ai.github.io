Maryam Khalid

Electronic and Information Engineering  ·  Nanjing, China
SHE Wearable Intelligent Monitoring Laboratory

I am an MSc researcher at Southeast University working at the intersection of physiological signal processing, federated learning, and wearable sensing. My research focuses on developing privacy-preserving multimodal frameworks for clinical AI applications, with a particular interest in designing systems that can function reliably in realistic, distributed healthcare settings.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Physiological Signal Processing Federated Learning Multimodal Wearable Sensing Deep Learning Biomedical AI EEG & ECG Analysis Clinical Decision Support Privacy-Preserving AI

My work sits at the convergence of wearable instrumentation, multimodal signal fusion, and federated machine learning. I am particularly interested in designing AI systems that operate under realistic clinical constraints: heterogeneous data distributions, missing modalities, and cross-site privacy requirements. Alongside signal-level work, I maintain an interest in medical imaging and its role in supporting diagnostic workflows.
